Not quite as groundbreaking as Wang Computers’ word processor, XtremeNotebooks is offering the first Quad-Core Intel processors in laptops at a price of over $10,000 per unit fully stocked. The XTreme 917 has a Core 2 Quad Q6000 processor with dual NVidia processors and almost 1TB of hard drive space, which means that unless this has a car battery inside, you’ll be looking at some dismal on-the-go use life. \[[XtremeNotebooks](http://www.xtremenotebooks.com/) via [PRNewswire](http://www.prnewswire.com/cgi-bin/stories.pl?ACCT=109&STORY=/www/story/08-15-2007/0004646288&EDATE=) via *Wired* (link is no longer active)\]
