Well it has been almost exactly a [month since we started](/physics/water-weenies-and-crystals/) growing the “Alum” crystals, and about two weeks since we [harvested the first one](/first-dangerous-crystal/) – now we have a complete first batch. Looks like someone’s struck it rich with a pocket full of diamonds …
