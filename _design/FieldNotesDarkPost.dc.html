The Seven Stages of Job Satisfaction

The mental stages job seekers go through over time, and the main factors that influence movement and decisions.

Bruce Abernethy November 13, 2024 10 minute read Grand Rapids, MI

At times in my career, I have been blessed to be “In The Zone” and seemingly working in my sweet spot—my “dream job.” Doing exciting and fulfilling things with other amazing people, having success, and meeting my personal and professional goals.

But seasons change, challenges come, and I develop a “Wandering Eye,” or in a few cases, had to change as the job was changing or ending.

Over time, especially as I got into some technical and people leadership roles, I saw some patterns emerge.

Stage 1 — “In The Zone”

You are doing work that matters with people you respect. Growth is happening without you forcing it. This is the stage people describe later as the best job I ever had, usually after they have left it.

The trap here is assuming it is permanent. It never is—and knowing that early changes how you treat it.

Stage 2 — “Content-ish”

Loyal but slightly dissatisfied. Nothing is wrong exactly, and that is precisely what makes this stage so easy to sit in for years.
